Scanbird support

Scan, sign and send documents from your iPhone.

Questions or problems? Email david-nilsson@live.se and we'll get back to you.

Restore your subscription

On a new iPhone, open Scanbird, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel your subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Scanbird. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends to avoid the next charge.

Keep your documents safe

In Scanbird, go to Settings and tap Back Up to iCloud Drive, then choose or create a folder. Scanbird keeps a PDF of every document there and updates it when you change a document.

Open a PDF from Mail or Files in Scanbird

Tap the share button on the PDF and choose Scanbird. It opens in the scan preview. Save it, and you can sign, redact or share it.
