Scanbird

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: Scanbird doesn't collect your data. Your scans stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we have no servers that could receive your documents.

Who we are

Scanbird is made by David Nilsson in Sweden ("we", "us"). For the little personal data we do receive, described below, we are the data controller. You can reach us at david-nilsson@live.se.

Your documents stay on your iPhone

Scanning, cropping, filters, text recognition, automatic document names, signatures, redaction, folders and search all run on your iPhone. Your documents, page images, recognized text, signatures and folder names are stored in Scanbird on your device. We never receive them.

Features that use Apple services

  • Translation. Translating text uses Apple's built-in translation. Apple provides it under Apple's Privacy Policy. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Backup to iCloud Drive. If you turn it on in Settings, Scanbird saves a PDF of each document in the folder you choose. If that folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores the PDFs in your iCloud account. We have no access to them.
  • iPhone backups. Depending on your iPhone settings, iCloud Backup or a computer backup may include Scanbird's data. That backup is between you and Apple.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and passcode lock. If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. Scanbird only learns whether unlocking succeeded. We never receive biometric data.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple. Scanbird learns from the App Store whether your subscription is active, not your name, email address or payment details.
  • Ratings. The rating prompt is Apple's own.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan documents, and only when you start a scan.
  • Photos: when you import from Photos, iOS shows a picker, and Scanbird only receives the photos you pick.
  • Files: Scanbird only opens files you choose, or files you open in Scanbird from another app.

When you share

When you share, email or export a document, it goes to the app, service or person you choose. Their own privacy policies then apply.

What we do receive

  • Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and delete it once it's no longer needed for that. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your question (GDPR article 6(1)(f)).
  • Crash reports. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. We use them only to fix problems.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, we don't sell or share personal data, and Scanbird contains no third-party code that collects data.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the support emails we have from you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, by writing to david-nilsson@live.se. If you think we handle personal data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or to the authority where you live.

Deleting your data

Delete a document in Scanbird to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of Scanbird's data from your iPhone. PDFs that Scanbird backed up to a folder you chose stay there until you delete them. To stop paying, cancel your subscription in the Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions.

Children

Scanbird isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly receive personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top. If a change matters for how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the app's update notes.
