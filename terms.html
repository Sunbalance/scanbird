4. Signatures

Scanbird places an image of your signature, initials or the date on a page. That is a simple electronic signature. Whether it's accepted for a particular document depends on the law and on the other party. Some documents need a qualified electronic signature, a handwritten signature or a witness, so check when it matters.

5. Redaction

A redaction blacks out its area, and the text under it, in the PDFs and images Scanbird exports. The page inside Scanbird keeps the original, so you can still move or remove the box. Only the area inside a box is hidden. Check each export before you send a sensitive document.

6. Text recognition, translation and names
